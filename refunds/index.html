Refund policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

If an application does not work for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. Include the email address used at checkout and, if you have it, the order id.

How to ask

In the app, open Settings → Licence → Request a refund. The licence is removed from that device first, and you are given a short code like RF-3F2A-9C1B — send it to support@siamcreations.app. If you would rather email first, send the order id from your Paddle receipt instead.

Payments are processed by Paddle as merchant of record, and refunds are issued through the same payment method.

Licence keys

A licence key is checked on your device with no account and no licence server, so it works offline. A refund cannot switch off an installation that never connects again. After a refund or chargeback we may add the key to a revocation list that the app reads the next time it starts with a connection, on Android or desktop. We may also decline further support for that licence. After the 14-day window, refunds are at our discretion. Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first — most problems are a configuration issue we can fix. A chargeback started without contact may lead to the associated licence being refused in future releases.
