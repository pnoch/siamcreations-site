Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Our applications are local-first. What you create stays on your device: there is no account required to use them, and no library server.

This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and ships no tracking scripts. It is served by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs, including your IP address, as any web host does.

What the applications store on your device

  • OmniHub: media files and metadata, notes, bookmarks, transcripts, playback progress, settings and your licence key.
  • OmniShop: your watchlist, alerts and price history.
  • OmniAuto: playback queue and position, preferences and a local diagnostic log.

None of this is uploaded by the applications. OmniHub's optional sync sends library data only to the address you enter on your own network. OmniShop's optional sync stores your watchlist on our sync service when you choose to sign in, and nowhere else.

What we receive

  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Paddle as merchant of record. We receive the buyer email, order id and the fact of payment — never card details.
  • Crash reports. Optional and off by default. When you enable them, a report carries the device model, OS version and a stack trace, never your library, filenames or notes.
  • Support messages. Whatever you choose to write to us.

Services you configure

Features that use a local AI endpoint (OmniHub's translation and subtitle generation) send requests directly to the endpoint you configure; we do not proxy them and do not receive their content. OmniShop requests prices from distributor listings when you refresh a watchlist.

Payments

Checkout is operated by Paddle, the merchant of record, which handles sales tax and VAT. Paddle's privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Your choices

Uninstalling an application removes its local data. To ask about purchase records or sync data, write to support; records required for tax and accounting are kept for the period the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.
