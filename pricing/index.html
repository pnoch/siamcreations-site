Before you buy

  • Ten-minute trial. Both licences unlock the same ten minutes of active use first. Time only counts while the app is open.
  • One key, one device. Open OmniHub on the device you want to use, copy the device code it shows, and paste it at checkout. The key is issued for that device, so it cannot be shared or resold.
  • Activate within 48 hours. A new key has to be activated on its device within two days of purchase. After that the deadline is gone for good — it never applies to a working licence.
  • Fully offline. After that, the app never contacts a licence server: the key is checked on the device and works for as long as you keep it.
  • Offline, with a weekly check. After activation the app works without a connection. While online it re-checks about once a week, which is how a refunded or blocked licence stops.
  • Tax. Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax or VAT, which is calculated at checkout by Paddle, our merchant of record.
  • Refunds. Ask within 14 days of purchase, from the address you paid with — see the refund policy.
  • Other products. OmniShop and OmniAuto are in development and free to try; their licence prices will be published here when they are released.
