About

Siamcreations is a small independent studio. We build the tools we wanted to use: an offline media library, a hardware price radar, an in-car companion. Every question that reaches us is answered by someone who works on the software.

How we build

The same three rules behind every product, because they are the reasons these tools exist at all.

Local first

What you create is stored on your device: libraries and notes work on a plane, and a watchlist survives an app restart. Only the lookups that need the network require it.

Your data stays yours

Libraries, notes and progress live on your device. There is no account to create and no library to upload.

Buy once

Where we sell licences they are one-time and checked on the device: no account, and a key is issued for the one device whose code you enter at checkout. Need it on a second device? Ask us and we will sort it out.

Support

Questions, licence problems, refunds — write to us. A person answers, usually within two working days.
